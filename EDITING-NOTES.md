# Editing notes

[← Course index](README.md)

Each lecture has its own page, with the original slides and the matching cleaned English transcript. No translation or study-guide summary has been added.

- Passages were aligned using slide text, illustrations, topic changes and phrases such as “on the right” or “next.” The source transcripts do not provide slide timestamps, so boundaries are inferred and sometimes approximate.
- Fillers such as “um,” “uh” and “mm,” and immediate repeated false starts, were removed. The lecturer’s explanations and examples were retained.
- Technical names were corrected against the slides and lecture context. `[Brackets]` indicate reconstructed missing words or uncertainty; `[unclear]` means the supplied material did not support a confident correction. This is a text-based correction, not an audio-verified verbatim transcript.
- A slide without a distinct new passage remains visible and links to the nearby discussion. A missing passage was not replaced with invented narration.
- Factual discrepancies noticed during editing appear as separate editorial notes. Historical species counts, phylogenies and biosecurity distributions remain lecture content; this is not a comprehensive update of the science.
- Each lecture includes its unchanged original transcript and a correction record. The searchable slide-text companion is an automatic extraction and may miss image-only labels.
