## Slide 5

[View slide 5](README.md#slide-5)

```text
Ephemeroptera (mayflies)
• >3,000 spp. worldwide
• aquatic immatures (nymphs/naiads)
• important for water filtering
• used in water quality analyses (= bioindicators)
• direct copulation
• non-feeding adults (vestigial or no mouthparts)
• short lived adults; 30 mins – 2 weeks
• higher-level phylogeny, classification unresolved
```
