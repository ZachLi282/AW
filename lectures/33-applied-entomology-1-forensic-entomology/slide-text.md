[View slide 6](README.md#slide-6)

```text
Forensic entomology
History of forensic entomology
• Slowly became a scientific branch in 13th-19th Century
• 1050 - first documented case in China
• 1668 - maggots were “found” to develop from eggs
laid by flies
• 1775 - first forensically important flies were
described, i.e. Calliphora vomitoria (Linnaeus, 1758)
```

Forensic entomology
1850 - the first legal case
• Mummified baby found in a chimney
by new tenants
• Tenants accused and arrested
• Autopsy revealed Sarcophaga carnaria
larvae and several moths
• Entomologist concluded body was
colonised by moths two years earlier
• New tenants were exonerated and
previous tenants were arrested for
murder
• Forensic entomology used ever since
