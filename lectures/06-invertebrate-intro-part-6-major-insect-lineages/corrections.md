[Slide 4](README.md#slide-4): The source generalises about two wing pairs and describes external fertilisation. Wing loss and reduction occur, and indirect transfer via spermatophores is not equivalent to fertilising eggs after laying.

[Slide 16](README.md#slide-16): Decticous/adecticous distinguish functional versus nonfunctional mandibles, not merely whether mouthparts are visible. Exarate/obtect distinguish free versus closely attached appendages. The source’s simplified wording is retained.
