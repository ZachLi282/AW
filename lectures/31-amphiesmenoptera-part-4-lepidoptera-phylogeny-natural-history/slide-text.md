## Slide 19

[View slide 19](README.md#slide-19)

```text
Plant / herbivore interactions
● Plant defense systems include (War et al. 2012)
● Structural barriers (hairs, thorns, hardening, …)
● Toxic chemicals
● Attraction of natural insect enemies
● Most moth species have overcome specific barriers and are host-specific
● Monophagous – just one host species
● Oligophagous – limited number of host species, often within one plant family
● Some moth species have overcome many different chemical defenses
● Polyphagous – many, often very different host species
● Most herbivorous caterpillars feed on leaves, but some on fruits, seeds, bark, timber, roots.
```
