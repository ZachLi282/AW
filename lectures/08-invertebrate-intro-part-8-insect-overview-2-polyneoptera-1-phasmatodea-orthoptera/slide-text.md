Phasmatodea
• stick insects, walking sticks; leaf insects; or
Phasmida- phytophagous - Lord Howe Isl.
• >3,000 described species
• can partially regenerate appendages
• Defenses: crypsis, color, spines, autotomy,
regurgitation, exocrine glands
• Reproduction: long mating or parthenogenesis;
eggs mimic seeds
