## Slide 3

[View slide 3](README.md#slide-3)

```text
Phylum Tardigrada
CRYPTOBIOSIS
• desiccation, secrete envelope of cuticle around
body (“tun”)
• almost total suspension of biological activity
(none detectable)
• tun can survive +149 to -272 degrees C, vacuum,
ionising radiation, no oxygen
• recorded survival over 120yr in museum sample
• survival on outside of orbiting satellite
```

## Slide 5

[View slide 5](README.md#slide-5)

```text
Phylum Tardigrada
MORPHOLOGY
• 4 lobopod pairs
• “toes” with adhesive pads
• chitin cuticle
• eutely in some species
• growth by moulting (3 to 6 instars)
• weak cephalisation (cerebral ganglion)
• long, sensory “cirri”
```
