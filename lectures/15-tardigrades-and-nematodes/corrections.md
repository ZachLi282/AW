[Slide 6](README.md#slide-6): Eutely concerns a fixed cell complement in particular tissues or life stages; the blanket claim that these animals cannot repair any damage is an oversimplification.

[Slide 30](README.md#slide-30): The 959-cell figure refers to adult somatic cells in the C. elegans hermaphrodite, not simply a female. Germline cell divisions are an important exception to the lecture’s generalisation.
