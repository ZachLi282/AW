```text
Diptera
• ~157 families; >160,000 species;
15% of all animals
• ecologically most diverse insect
order: bacteria, phytophagous,
saprophagous, fungivorous,
predatory, parasitic, parasitoid
• blood feeding disease vectors -
most important medical and
veterinary insects
• crop, livestock, urban pests
• many parasitoids, including
hypermetamorphosis
• beneficial - pollinators, biocontrol,
major decomposers
• no ovipositor - oviscapt
```

```text
Diptera Summary
Morphologically disparate
Ecologically labile between and w/in families
Saprophagy, fungivory, plant feeding,
parasitism incl. bloodfeeding, vertebrates
3 major radiations
Lower Diptera: Tipulidae, Ceratopogonidae, Chironomidae, Culicidae,
Psychodidae, Mycetophilidae, Cecidomyiidae
Lower Brachycera: Tabanidae, Stratiomyidae, Asilidae, Bombyliidae,
Empididae, Dolichopodidae, Phoridae, Syrphidae
Schizophora: Tephritidae, Chloropidae, Agromyzidae, Drosophilidae,
Muscidae, Calliphoridae, Sarcophagidae, Tachinidae, Pupipara
Close relatives: Mecoptera scorpionflies, Siphonaptera fleas
```
