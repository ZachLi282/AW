[View slide 14](README.md#slide-14)

```text
Parasitoid Interactions
Superparasitism: laying a clutch of egg(s) on a host already parasitized by the same
species, only 1 larva can survive.
Multiparasitism: host parasitized by 2 or more species of parasitoids (more
common than superparasitism). Also competition! Sometimes all survive=
gregarious
Hyperparasitism: occurrence of a parasitoid on another parasite or parasitoid
(can be ecto- or endo-). Bad for pest management.
Eggs are injected on
leaves, eaten by
caterpillar
Attack parasitoids that
attack caterpillar
Trigonalidae
```
