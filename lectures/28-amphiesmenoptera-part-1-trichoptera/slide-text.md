## Slide 7

[View slide 7](README.md#slide-7)

```text
Trichoptera (caddisflies)
•
About 10,500 spp. in 45 families worldwide,
but only 852 spp. in 27 families in Australia.
•
Aquatic immatures (egg, larva, pupa), almost exclusively freshwater,
but marine Chathamiidae (5 spp.: AU, NZ) and
terrestrial Limnephilidae & Apataniidae (<20 spp.: Holarctic)
•
Terrestrial adults:
●1 - 43 mm long, often plain-looking,
●densely covered in hairs,
●mouth parts form a haustellum [SYNAPO],
●antennae always held forward.
```

```text
SUMMARY
●Amphiesmenoptera = Trichoptera + Lepidoptera
● Larva with spinneret for silk production (SYNAPO).
●Trichoptera
●Small order with 10,500 / 852 species globally / in AU.
●Aquatic larvae, most living in portable cases or fixed silken shelters.
●Larvae are characterised by having gills and abdominal claws.
●Terrestrial adults, winged.
●Adults with haustellum (SYNAPO) to feed on nectar / liquids.
●Adults relatively densely clothed in hairs.
```
