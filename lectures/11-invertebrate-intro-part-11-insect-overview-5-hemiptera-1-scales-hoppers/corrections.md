[Slide 3](README.md#slide-3): Laciniae are parts of the maxillae, not the labium. The source’s anatomical claim is retained.

[Slide 7](README.md#slide-7): Many aphids give birth to live young in parthenogenetic generations, as the slide shows; the transcript’s generalisation about laying eggs is retained as a lecture claim.

[Slide 12](README.md#slide-12): Many adult male scales have reduced or nonfunctional mouthparts. The source’s proposed sucking-proboscis distinction from flies is not a general identification rule.

[Slide 18](README.md#slide-18): Brochosomes are generally described as protein-rich particles, not simply carbohydrate spheres. A major grape disease vectored by sharpshooters, Pierce’s disease, is caused by the bacterium Xylella fastidiosa rather than a virus. These are factual clarifications, not transcription substitutions.
